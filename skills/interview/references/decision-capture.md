# Decision capture

Use this guidance when the user asks for a durable record of the interview, or
when the project maintains a glossary, decision records, or a specification
that the interview changes.

## Choose the destination

Use the project's existing documents, locations, and formats. An interview does
not by itself authorize creating files. When the user asks for a record and no
convention exists, propose a location, or use a single summary document in the
project's documentation location when the request already covers it.

## Record as decisions settle

Within authorized documentation work, update the record after the round in which
a decision settles rather than only at the end. This keeps the record usable if
the interview stops early. Without that authorization, keep the record in the
conversation summary.

## Record the substance

- the decision and the reason that matters;
- exact constraints as the user stated them, such as limits, names, and
  boundaries;
- rejected alternatives when their rationale is likely to matter again;
- delegated decisions, marked as delegated, with the choice and its reason;
- open questions and the work they block.

Keep proposals, recommendations, and decisions distinct. Mark a decision as
accepted only when the user or the responsible owner has made it.

## Terminology

When the interview resolves the meaning of a domain term, update the existing
glossary within authorization. Define the term in a sentence or two and keep
implementation detail out of the definition.

## Separate decision records

Propose a separate decision record when the rationale would be difficult to
reconstruct and the decision has lasting consequences. Strong signals include
reversal cost, a constraint that would surprise a later reader, and a meaningful
trade-off. Explicit project requirements or a significant operational obligation
can also justify a record.

Do not require all signals mechanically or propose a record for every routine
choice. A short entry in the summary or existing decision log may be sufficient.

Follow the project's existing record format, numbering, and status conventions.
A proposed record does not authorize implementation.
